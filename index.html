Research Directions

World Models

Learning predictive latent dynamics that let agents imagine, plan, and reason about consequences before acting.

Model-Based RL

TD-MPC2, MuZero, Dreamer and beyond — sample-efficient control through planning in learned models.

Embodied Manipulation

Bringing learned world models to real robotic systems: manipulation, sim-to-real, and closed-loop control.

Representation Learning

JEPA-style objectives and value-aware representations — what should a model keep, and what can it forget?

About

I'm an independent researcher working on embodied intelligence. My conviction is simple: agents that understand the world through models will outperform agents that merely react to it.

My current work centers on world-model-based decision making — reading, reproducing, and extending ideas from the TD-MPC2 / MuZero / Dreamer line of research, with the goal of making these methods work on physical robots.

Previously in industry robotics; now researching independently and writing in the open.

Currently Thinking About

  • Value-equivalent world models — models that are wrong in the right ways
  • Test-time search — how much planning should an agent do on the spot?
  • Critics as implicit world models — what does a value function already know?
  • Hierarchical planning — skills, subgoals, and temporal abstraction
  • Offline model-based RL — learning to act without touching the world
